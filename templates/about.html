About the osu! Malaysia Top 15

Welcome to the official community-driven Top 15 voting for osu! Malaysia! This event celebrates the best players in our country based on the community's consensus.

How the Point System Works

When you submit a ballot, your #1 pick receives 15 points, your #2 pick receives 14 points, down to your #15 pick receiving 1 point.

Anti-Bias Measure (Trimmed Rankings): To prevent extreme outlier votes from skewing the results, we generate a "Trimmed" ranking in the final spreadsheet. This drops the highest and lowest points awarded to every player before calculating the final average.

Example: If a player receives: 1, 5, 2, 5, 3, 1, 9, 10, 10, 7, 6. The 1 (lowest) and 10 (highest) are removed, and the rest are summed for a more accurate representation.
